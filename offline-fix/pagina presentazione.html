HOW THE GAME WORKS

The game is set in a maze made up of corridors and rooms, where the player controls — using the arrow keys or WASD — Eda Ucak, a beautiful artist who will courageously fight against the abuses of the artificial minds that are trying to make us all stupid. The goal of the game is to have Eda eat all the Kartoffeln scattered around the maze, while avoiding the "AI" that are trying to capture Eda Ucak and destroy her art.

There are also three special "big Kartoffeln," placed in the corners of the maze — when Eda eats one, the AIs become vulnerable for a short period of time (10 seconds), making them edible, easy peasy.

When Eda runs out of Kartoffeln, the game moves on to the next level, in which the ghosts will be 8% faster.

The goal of the game is to score as many points as possible before being eliminated three times by the American artificial intelligences and falling into the subscriptions of oblivion.
